How to play (60 seconds)

  1. Manager layer: hire up to 12 players, keep 7 fit. Big Guys cost more. Heal bruises, buy re-rolls, train with SPP.
  2. Match layer: turn-based. Each of your 7 players acts once per team-turn: move up to MA squares, plus one action (block / blitz / pass / handoff).
  3. Turnover: if you fail a dodge, drop the ball, fumble a pass or fall over — your turn ends immediately. That's Blood Bowl.
  4. Blocking: compare Strength. More ST = more block dice. Results: 💥POW (down), ✨Stumble (down unless Dodge), ➡️Push, 🔄Both Down, 💀Skull (you fall).
  5. Ball: run it into the red endzone to score. Pick up (AG roll), pass (AG roll by range), catch (AG roll).
  6. Injuries: beaten armour (2D6 ≥ AV) → injury roll: Stun / KO / Badly Hurt / Miss Next Game / ☠ Dead.
  7. Season: 3 games vs AI + table. Win = 3pts, Draw = 1pt. Earn gold + fans, get MVP & SPP, buy stars.
  8. Like FM: check your Inbox, set Tactics mentality (Attacking +1 MA / −1 AV, Cautious the reverse), and keep board confidence above zero — or get sacked.
  9. Mega-corps: Corpo crews are insured (+50k per death) and sponsored (+15k gate every match).
  10. Chaos gods: Khorne (+1 ST on blitzes), Nurgle (KOs become stuns), Tzeentch (+5k per completed pass), Slaanesh (+5k & +1 fan per TD), Horned Rat Skaven (+1 MA for all).
  11. Economy: players draw weekly wages, as do hired staff (Ripperdoc heals, Coach trains). Broke? Borrow from loan sharks — mind the interest and your credit rating.
  12. Guns: every ganger can shoot instead of acting — 4+ short, 5+ long, pinned on any hit, standing blockers stop bullets, prone bodies give cover. Rolling a 1 on ammo jams the gun for the match. No turnovers from gunfire.
  13. Match depth: 8 turns/half, weather (heat/blizzard sap MA, sun ruins long passes, rain soaks catches), kickoff omens (invasions, free re-rolls), assists (+ST per unmarked helper, max 2), sprints (+2 squares, fall on a 1), and the ball never rests on a downed player.
  14. New houses: Krakens salvage armour (+3k/break), Guild renders the dead (+25k/death), Volt re-rolls cost 40k, Choir gains +2 fans per win.
  15. Hive gangs: Venom bounties (+5k/casualty), Ash (+10k/TD), Ghost (+10k/match), Forge (+10k/win), Static (free re-roll each match), Rust (+2 fans per death).
