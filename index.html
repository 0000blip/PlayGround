Prologue — The Mud Remembers

New Orleans does not yet exist on any map the King will sign. In 1666 there is only a wound in the canebrake: a private seigneurie staked by the Compagnie des Marais, palisades around a chapel that was never consecrated, a manor built of stolen cypress, and sixty souls in debt for the price of their crossing.

You are Élodie / Étienne Marchand, indentured, coffin-ship survivor, reader of forbidden pamphlets — a believer that no man owns another, neither by deed, nor by debt, nor by God. The pamphlet called it the commune of the dead and the living. The bayou is about to test the thesis.

The priest says the dead walk because of sin. The Seigneur says they walk because the workers are lazy. Old Manon of the bayou says they walk because they were never paid.

Survive. Eat. Barricade. Choose. Every loaf shared or stolen, every throat cut or spared, is weighed on the scales of the Requiem. The swamp keeps ledgers better than any taxman.

🩸 Project-Zomboid rules

Eat before hunger empties or HP bleeds. Sleep? There is no sleep here — only the bell. Barricade doors with planks (C). Boiled herbs cure bites. Forage the October woods (E at green growth: pecans, muscadines, persimmons, groundnuts…), hunt rabbit, turkey and deer, beware alligators, cottonmouths, hogs and the old black bear — yaupon leaves steady the nerves. Horses can be mounted (E); carts driven (E) and used as a shared stash (V). Night is death: lantern, fire and chapel ground save you. Death drops your satchel where you fell (recover the ⚰ mark). Claim a cottage (E at its door) as your safehouse: wake there, store goods in its chest, sleep in its bed, and bar its doorway with bundles.

⚖ Karma

Share bread +3 · bury the dead +4 · free quests +5…+10 · murder −25 · theft −8 · lying −4 · eating the Seigneur's store in front of the poor +2. Saints get discounts, allies and chapel sanctuary. Butchers are hunted — and the Bête learns their name.

🕯 Reason (sanity)

Every horror gnaws: Risen, Loups, Apparitions, Sang-Mêlé, the Bête itself — worse at night, worse wounded. Lanterns, fires and chapel ground halve the gnawing. Recover by sleeping in bunks/pews/hammocks (E, dusk to dawn), drinking wine & tafia, smoking pipe tobacco, confessing to Père Anselme, or company with Dame Séraphine. Below 30, the dark starts talking back; at 0 it starts collecting.

⚒ Work & professions

Fourteen honest (and-ish) trades: carpenter, cook, pirate, fisherman, hunter, sheriff, soldier, tavern keeper, smith, farmer, banker, tycoon, sewer cleaner, town cryer. Find their stations by floating signs, press E to work a shift: coin, trade goods, +🕯 sanity, and XP toward Apprentice → Grandmaster (better pay, deeper peace). Smiths need planks; tycoons need a stake; sewers risk the miasma. Press B for the ledger. Every trade has a quest.

✨ Conjure (voodoo)

Spirit (🌀) trickles back slowly; rituals at 4 altars (crossroads, cemetery, bayou, chapel — bring offerings) restore floods. Cast from the conjure-book (R) or keys 7, 8, 9, 0, –: ward, holy light, haste, communion, rebuke. Craft a gris-gris (C) for +20 max. Manon teaches the work.

🐺 The Five Plagues

Risen — slow, bite infects. Loup-Garou — fleet by moon, silver & fire. Apparitions — drain reason (sanity); blessed steel & lantern hurt them. Sang-Mêlé (vampires) — proud, parley at dusk, burn at dawn. Bête de l'Abîme — the demon of the drained swamp. Boss of the Leveling Night.
